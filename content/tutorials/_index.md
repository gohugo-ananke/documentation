Tutorials guide you through a practical exercise. Start here when you are new to Ananke and want to learn by building something.

## Build your first site

[Build your first Ananke site](/tutorials/first-site/) takes you from installation to a homepage, a post, navigation, and a local production build. You can follow Hugo's existing Ananke lesson, or use either of our installation methods and continue with the local exercise.

## After the tutorial

* Use [How-to guides](/how-to/) to complete a particular task on your own site.
* Read [Explanation](/explanation/) to understand configuration and customisation choices.
* Consult [Reference](/reference/) for settings, front matter, shortcodes, and hooks.
