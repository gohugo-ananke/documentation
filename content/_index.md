* **[Configuration](/configuration/)**: set site parameters, menus, SEO, social links, and languages.
* **[Content](/content/)**: write pages and posts with front matter, images, taxonomies, and shortcodes.
* **[Customisation](/customisation/)**: change colours and styles, override partials, and use hooks & filters.
* **[Examples and recipes](/examples/)**: copy complete site configurations and short solutions.
* **[Help and community](/help/)**: fix common problems, report issues, and contribute.
