Ananke reads its settings from your Hugo site configuration and from the front matter of your pages. The pages in this section explain each group of settings with complete examples, default values, and required or optional status.

## Find a topic

* **Set up a new site:** start with [General configuration](/configuration/general/), then set up the [Homepage](/configuration/homepage/) and the [Menus](/configuration/menus/).
* **Change the header image or title area:** read [Page headers](/customisation/hero-section/).
* **Show links to your social profiles:** read [Social media networks](/configuration/social-media-networks/).
* **Improve search engine results and link previews:** read [SEO](/configuration/seo/) and [robots.txt and environments](/configuration/robots/).
* **Track visitors:** read [Analytics](/configuration/analytics/).
* **Publish in more than one language:** read [Multilingual and i18n](/configuration/multilingual/).
* **Look up a parameter:** use the [Configuration reference](/configuration/reference/).
