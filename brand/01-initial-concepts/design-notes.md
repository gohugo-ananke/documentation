# Ananke logo concepts

## Brief and assumptions

Ananke is a theme for Hugo. Audience assumed: writers, developers, and maintainers of content-led websites. Desired qualities assumed: readable, dependable, adaptable, and editorial. Primary contexts: documentation header, README, browser tab, and social avatar. No palette or existing visual equity was specified. These are exploratory concepts, not approved brand assets.

## Exploration

Concepts considered (clarity, distinction, simplicity, relevance, and small-size strength; qualitative assessment):

1. Open A: a deliberately interrupted initial; selected for immediate name recognition and a strong silhouette.
2. Interleave: two offset bands forming a continuous visual rhythm; selected as an abstract alternative.
3. Imprint: a compact lowercase a with an open upper counter; selected for its editorial character and small-size clarity.
4. Orbital A: an initial interrupted by a circular orbit; rejected because the orbit adds detail without identifying the theme.
5. Woven AN: overlapping initials; rejected because the junctions become dense at favicon sizes.
6. Margin: a page edge with an A-shaped gap; rejected because the page becomes the dominant reading.
7. Keystone: a triangle assembled from three blocks; rejected as too close to generic framework symbols.
8. Running head: a wordmark with a repeated editorial rule; rejected because the rule weakens at small sizes.
9. Folded folio: an open book with an A in its spine; rejected as too literal for publishing.
10. Thread: a single loop ending in an a; rejected because the thin path loses weight at small sizes.

Category conventions examined through the skill's framework/library collection. Studied the construction of Framer, Pagekit, and Prisma for simple silhouettes and negative space, without tracing. Avoided code brackets, generic lightning bolts, and Hugo-style hexagon treatments.

## Construction and refinement

All symbols use a 256-unit square, flat near-black fill, and simple closed paths. Open A uses a flat apex and an open counter. Interleave uses repeated 45-degree segments. Imprint uses a curved bowl, a horizontal upper terminal, and an upright stem.

The second pass replaces Open A's temporary white knockout with a true open contour and recentres Imprint. The three second-pass symbols pass the skill's structural SVG audit. The overview shows true 64, 32, and 16 px examples. Inspect tests.html for backgrounds, mirror/rotation views, and category references.

Lockup typography is exploratory DejaVu Sans Bold for A/B and DejaVu Serif Bold for C, converted to paths with Inkscape. The symbols are original path drawings. Wordmark customisation and a final font licence record belong to the approved-direction stage.

No trademark clearance has been performed. Recommendation: Imprint; risk: an isolated lowercase a needs the Ananke wordmark to establish recognition. Open A risks a generic technology reading; Interleave does not immediately communicate the name.
